# Uber-Sales-Analysis

🚖 Uber Auto Dashboard | Power BI Project

Overview
📌 This Power BI dashboard analyzes Uber Auto ride data to track bookings, revenue, ride outcomes, distance traveled, and customer and driver satisfaction. The objective of this project is to convert raw ride data into actionable business insights through interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.

Business Problem
❗ Ride-hailing operations generate large volumes of data, but without structured analysis it becomes difficult to:

* Identify booking losses and cancellation patterns
* Understand revenue contribution by vehicle type
* Track seasonal demand fluctuations
* Monitor customer and driver satisfaction
* Make quick, data-driven operational decisions

This dashboard addresses these challenges by consolidating key metrics into a single, interactive view.

Key KPIs
💰 Total Revenue: 52M
📦 Total Bookings: 30K
✅ Completed Bookings: 19K
❌ Lost Bookings: 11K
📏 Total Distance Covered: 24.61

Insights and Analysis

📈 Booking Performance
Monthly completed bookings remain largely stable throughout the year, with noticeable dips in the early months and higher demand toward Q4. This helps the business anticipate peak periods and plan driver availability accordingly.

💸 Revenue Contribution
Go Mini is the highest revenue-generating vehicle type with a contribution of 10.3M, enabling the business to focus marketing and supply strategies on high-demand, high-return services.

🔄 Ride Status Distribution
Visual breakdown of completed, incomplete, and cancelled rides highlights operational inefficiencies and potential revenue leakage points.

📍 Top Locations
🚕 Top Pickup Location: Jasola (134 rides)
🏁 Top Drop Location: Kashmere Gate ISBT (139 rides)
These insights help optimize driver positioning and reduce passenger wait times.

⭐ User Experience
🙂 Average Customer Rating: 4.44
🚗 Average Driver Rating: 4.19
Consistently high ratings indicate strong service quality while still leaving scope for targeted improvement programs.

Interactive Features
🎛 Month and Quarter slicers
🚘 Vehicle type filters including Auto, Bike, eBike, Go Mini, Uber XL, and others
📊 Dynamic KPI cards and trend-based visualizations

Tech Stack
🛠 Power BI
📐 DAX measures
🧩 Data modeling
🎨 Interactive dashboard design

Business Impact from Visuals
📊 Enables leadership to quickly assess overall business performance at a glance
📉 Helps reduce revenue loss by identifying high cancellation and incomplete ride patterns
📅 Supports demand forecasting using monthly and seasonal booking trends
🚕 Improves fleet and driver allocation using pickup and drop location insights
⭐ Strengthens customer experience by monitoring satisfaction scores
